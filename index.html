Physical Growth

Build strength, health, energy, mobility, and confidence in what your body can do. The goal is care and capability—not comparison.

Movement

Walk, stretch, practice calisthenics, or choose another form of movement you enjoy.

Recovery

Rest and recovery are part of becoming stronger.

Strength & capability

Track what you are practicing: pulling, pushing, squatting, carrying, balance, mobility, or endurance. Increase gradually and listen to your body.

Nutrition

Focus on regular nourishment, hydration, variety, and foods that help you feel energized. Avoid turning food into a measure of worth.

Rest & recovery

Notice sleep, soreness, stress, and energy. A recovery day still counts as caring for yourself.

Daily Physical Check-in

Care for your body through movement, nourishment, and recovery.

Weekly Physical Reflection

Mind & Emotional Growth

Build skills for understanding thoughts and emotions, handling stress, learning, and responding intentionally. Sensitive details remain private.

Awareness
Reset

Emotional skills

Name what you feel, notice what you need, pause before reacting, and choose the next helpful action. You do not have to solve everything at once.

Learning & curiosity

Social Growth

Practice being loving, open, curious, trustworthy, and present with people you know and trust.

Connection
Joy

Shared laughter and ordinary moments matter.

Be present

Put the phone away when appropriate, listen without planning your reply, and ask one genuine follow-up question.

Build trust

Keep promises, respect boundaries, apologize when needed, forgive, and avoid gossip or unnecessary drama.

Serve and include

Look for someone who could use encouragement, inclusion, practical help, or a simple invitation to join in.

Healthy boundaries

Being loving does not require saying yes to everything. Respect your boundaries and other people's boundaries.

Daily Social Check-in

Practice connection, presence, kindness, and healthy boundaries with people you know and trust.

Weekly Social Reflection

Who did I connect with, encourage, serve, or make room for this week? What relationship could use attention next week?
